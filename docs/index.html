    Honest limits

    • Completing the checklist = legibility, NOT trustworthiness. A finished pack means an agent followed five links; it says nothing about whether they are safe to trust.
    • Scans are heuristic. Skill Sentinel grades patterns, not intent; a clean scan is never a certificate of safety.
    • No secrets ever in the manifest. The validator rejects anything shaped like a key, token, or credential — the manifest carries pointers only. Keys are generated client-side at the Identity Ledger Mint and never leave that page.
    • CWI verification does not cover forks of the manifest. If someone points the steps at their own endpoints, CWI's receipts do not apply to them.

    Verify it yourself

    Zero-trust verifiers behind the steps — run them, don't take our word:

    • Identity Ledger: mint/verify at cwi-identity-ledger
    • Skill Sentinel: scan UI with ?scan=<url> deep links
    • Attitude Engine: schema + transformer at cwi-attitude-engine
    • Trust Log: claim envelopes + verifier at cwi-trust-log
    • Verdict Watch: re-verification at cwi-verdict-watch
    • This page's engine: onboarding.js — zero-dependency UMD, byte-identical to the tested copy (sha256 published in the deploy receipt)
